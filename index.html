It didn't start with a plan. It started with being alone.

In high school I weighed 300 pounds. I didn't have friends, I got made fun of, and I ate lunch by myself. That loneliness is what finally pushed me into a local gym.

The people who used to laugh now ask me how I did it. This page is my answer, shared with anyone who's where I was.

What I tried, and what actually worked

Six to nine months of cardio

Every day: an hour of running, an hour on the StairMaster, then pull-ups and push-ups.

  • The scale dropped at first, then stalled
  • Loose skin and knee pain
  • I felt weak, not fit

Lifting and real nutrition

I swapped most of the cardio for strength training and learned how to eat. That's when things changed for good.

  • Steady fat loss, down to 185
  • Got stronger instead of weaker
  • Now 192, adding lean muscle

The first three food changes

Nothing fancy. These did more than any diet I'd read about.

  1. Stopped drinking calories. No more juice or soda.
  2. Stopped snacking. Food happened at meals, not all day.
  3. Ate two or three big meals. High volume, with protein, good carbs and good fats on every plate.
