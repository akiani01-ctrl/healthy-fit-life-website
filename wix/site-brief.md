# Healthy Fit Life: Wix site brief

This is the brief used to generate the Healthy Fit Life coaching site in the Wix editor (Oct 21, 2026).

- Platform: Wix (AI site builder)
- Domain: none yet. Using the free Wix address (`*.wixsite.com/...`) for now; connect a custom domain later.

## Brand
- Name: Healthy Fit Life
- Tagline: "I went from 300 lbs to lean. Come on the journey with me."
- Business type: online fitness and nutrition coaching
- Faceless brand: brand name only, no personal name or coach photos.

## Target audience
Adults (mostly 18-40) who are overweight or stuck, have tried cardio and dieting without lasting results, and want a realistic plan to lose fat, build muscle and keep it off. Most visitors come from TikTok and Instagram, so the site is mobile-first.

## Style
- Dark, bold, motivating modern gym look
- Colors: black/charcoal backgrounds, white text, neon green `#39FF14` accents and buttons
- Fonts: bold sans-serif headings, clean readable body text
- Images: dark moody gym shots, high-protein meals, weights. No smiling stock-model photos.

## Pages

### 1. Home
- Hero: "From 300 lbs to lean. No gimmicks, just what actually worked."
- Subtext: "Real fat-loss and muscle-building coaching from someone who's been where you are."
- Buttons: "Start Your Journey" (to Coaching) and "Get the Free Guide" (email signup)
- "Sound familiar?": hours of cardio and the scale stops moving; drinking your calories without realizing it; feeling weak, tired and stuck
- "What changed everything" (3 cards): strength training over endless cardio; stop drinking your calories; 2-3 big high-protein meals instead of constant snacking
- Story teaser, coaching preview, email signup, TikTok and Instagram links

### 2. My Story
- 300 lbs in high school: no friends, made fun of, sat alone at lunch. That loneliness became the motivation.
- 1 hour running plus 1 hour StairMaster daily for 6-9 months. Lost weight at first, then stalled, with loose skin, knee pain and feeling weak.
- Switched to lifting and learned nutrition, which is what actually worked: cut juice and soda, stopped snacking, ate 2-3 high-volume meals with protein, good carbs and healthy fats.
- Over 2+ years went from 300 to 185 lbs. Now 192 lbs and building lean muscle.
- The people who mocked him now ask how he did it.
- Closing: "If I could do it, you can too. Let's do it together."

### 3. Coaching
- Starter Plan: custom nutrition guide + beginner strength program (one-time). Price: TBD
- 1:1 Online Coaching: personalized meal plan, training program, weekly check-ins, form feedback, direct messaging (monthly). Price: TBD
- Free Guide: "The 5 Changes That Dropped My First 50 lbs" (email signup)

### 4. Blog
Why cardio alone stalls fat loss · Easy high-protein food swaps · Meal timing basics · Fixing common lifting form mistakes · Fat-loss myths

### 5. Results
Placeholder for real client results. No fake testimonials.

### 6. FAQ
- Do I need a gym? (Gym preferred; home options available)
- I've failed before. Will this work for me?
- How fast will I see results?
- Do I have to give up foods I love?
- Can meal plans fit halal or vegetarian diets? (Yes)

### 7. Contact / Apply
Form: name, email, current weight, goal, biggest struggle, gym access (yes/no), which coaching option

## Features
Email signup for the free guide · online payments for coaching plans · booking for a free 15-minute intro call · blog · Instagram feed · contact/application form
